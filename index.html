MATHEMATICAL MODEL

Calculation & Theory

Mathematical equations used by the computational model.

EQUATION 01

Gyroscopic Couple

C = Iωωₚ

The gyroscopic couple is the product of moment of inertia, spin angular velocity and precession angular velocity.

EQUATION 02

Wheel Angular Velocity

ω = 2πN / 60

Converts wheel speed from RPM into radians per second.

EQUATION 03

Precession Angular Velocity

ωₚ = 2πNₚ / 60

Converts precession speed from RPM into radians per second.

EQUATION 04

Solid Disc

I = ½mR²

Moment of inertia of a solid disc about its central axis.

EQUATION 05

Thin Ring

I = mR²

Moment of inertia of a thin ring about its central axis.

FINAL MODEL

Computational Relation

C = I × (2πN/60) × (2πNₚ/60)

Final computational equation used by the analyzer.

01

Rigid Wheel

The rotating wheel is considered rigid and does not deform during rotation.

02

Constant Speed

Wheel speed and precession speed are assumed to remain constant.

03

Negligible Friction

Bearing friction and aerodynamic resistance are neglected.

04

Ideal Geometry

Mass is assumed to be uniformly distributed according to the selected geometry.

05

Small Precession Effects

The model considers the standard gyroscopic couple relation.

06

SI Units
